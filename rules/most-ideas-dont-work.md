# Most ideas don't work

**Why.** When teams actually test their ideas, most make no difference or make things worse. An idea feeling useful to the person who had it tells you very little.

**Example.** An animated header looks better, but I can't name anyone who'd do something differently because of it. A header showing the one command to try first might get someone to try it.
